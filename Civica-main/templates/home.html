About Us

CIVICA AI was created with a simple belief: access to correct information should not depend on language, literacy, or digital skills.

In today's digital world, millions of people still struggle to understand government programs, fall victim to fake news or online scams, and lack awareness about essential topics like nutrition and digital safety. CIVICA AI exists to bridge this gap.

By combining modern AI technology with a people-first approach, CIVICA AI aims to empower communities, strengthen public awareness, and support informed decision-making across Bharat.
